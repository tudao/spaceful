# Spaceful — Product Overview

## What is Spaceful?

Spaceful is a personal AI space builder. Users answer five questions and Claude generates a unique, animated personal page — their "space" — that reflects their personality, current goals, and aesthetic preferences.

A space is a living dashboard: it shows what the owner is working toward, what they're focused on this week, what they're reading, and what their current habits look like. It's half personal homepage, half weekly intention tracker.

Every space is accessible at `spaceful.io/:username`. The owner can edit it directly in the browser (click-to-edit, no forms), and visitors can leave a short reaction.

---

## The Five Questions

During onboarding, users answer:

1. **Name** — what to call the space (e.g. "Laki's World")
2. **Vibes** — up to 3 words from a curated list (calm, creative, focused, cozy, bold, playful, minimal, dreamy, grounded, energetic)
3. **Big goal** — their current most important goal, optional
4. **Colour mood** — one of six palettes (lavender, sand, forest, ocean, rose, midnight), with a live preview
5. **World** — a choice of base template (Garden, Cosmos, Ocean, Sky, Journal, Laki's World) or "Let AI surprise me". Includes a density toggle (spacious / rich) and auto-selects a **Space Companion archetype** based on mood + vibes.

Claude Haiku then generates a full design spec: layout, decoration type and density, card style, typography, colour palette, and personalised copy — all tailored to that specific combination of inputs.

## What a Space Contains

A space has **three tabs**, each with its own content:

### Space tab (default)
Sections chosen by the AI and editable by the owner:

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Header | Name, time-of-day greeting, tagline, monogram, goal completion % |
| Goals | Chip-style goal cards with done/undone toggle |
| Currently | A single "currently doing X" status badge |
| Focus hero | This week's focus card with title and notes; day-specific nudge on Mon/Fri |
| Notepad | Free-text area with free / checklist / bullets tabs |
| Kanban | Week-by-week card carousel (W1–W52), auto-scrolls to current week |
| Habit tracker | 7-day grid per habit; today's column highlighted |
| Daily Pulse | Morning intention + evening reflection, 2-sentence max each, streak counter |
| Reading list | Book/article cards with reading → queued → done cycling |
| Quote | Semantic quote matched to goal text via pgvector |
| Photo | A featured image with caption |
| Streak | A focused-days counter |

The AI picks 4–7 sections based on the user's layout preference and vibes.

### Journal tab
- **Full Daily Pulse timeline** — all morning/evening entries, newest first, paired by day
- **Monthly AI Letters** — pinned synthesis cards generated on the 1st of each month
- **Space Evolution timeline** — horizontal scroll of monthly snapshots showing how the space changed

### Companion tab (owner-only)
- **Daily message** — 3–5 sentence reflection generated by Claude Haiku using full space context (goals, habits, pulse entries). Generated lazily on first visit, cached all day. Free.
- **Semantic quote** — curated quote matched to goal text via pgvector similarity search
- **On-demand chat** — user asks the companion a question, streams a response (0.1 credits)

## Credits

Every action on Spaceful costs credits. Credits are a `numeric(6,2)` value — fractional costs exist.

| Action | Cost |
|---|---|
| Generate a new space | 3 credits |
| Regenerate theme | 2 credits |
| AI journaling prompt | 0.25 credits |
| OG image regeneration | 0.5 credits |
| Companion on-demand chat | 0.1 credits |
| Monthly AI Letter (free tier) | 0.5 credits (opt-in) |
| Daily Pulse entries | **Free** — daily ritual must never cost credits |
| Companion daily message | **Free** — retention feature, cached once per day |
| Remix a space layout | **Free** — acquisition mechanic |
| Weekly digest email | **Free** — external trigger |

New accounts receive 3 credits on signup (enough for one space). Credits are purchased via Stripe or granted via subscription renewal.

## Gallery

Spaces can be submitted to the public gallery by their owners. Gallery submission goes through a moderation queue. Approved spaces appear at `/gallery`, filterable by mood + vibe tag. The gallery doubles as the landing page hero.

**Space of the Week** — one curated space is featured on the landing page each week, chosen by admin. The featured space owner receives an email notification.

**One-click Remix** — logged-in visitors can copy any public space's template configuration (layout, decoration, card style) to their own space. Content is never copied. Source space receives `remix_count` increment and optional lineage credit.

## Visitor Knocks

Visitors can leave one of three knock types on any space with `reactions_enabled = true`:

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **message** | Short text (1–140 chars) |
| **energy** | One tap, no text, no account required. Shows as "✦ N energies" on the space |
| **goal_cheer** | Visitor picks a specific goal and writes a note encouraging it |

Rate limited to 3 per space per IP per hour (DB-based, serverless-safe). IP never stored — only `SHA256(ip + space_id)`. Owner approves/hides knocks. Owners receive a **weekly email digest** summarising new knocks and space stats.

## Space Companion

Each space has an AI companion with one of five archetypes chosen during onboarding:

| Archetype | Tone |
|---|---|
| The Stoic | Calm, honest, direct about hard truths |
| The Coach | Action-oriented, specific, energetic |
| The Poet | Reflective, metaphorical, gentle |
| The Sage | Philosophical, long view, patient |
| The Challenger | Cuts through excuses, high standards |

The companion reads full context — goals, habit completion rate, this week's focus, recent pulse entries, reading list, mood — and delivers a daily message. Archetype defaults are inferred from mood + vibes at generation time.

## Vision

Spaceful is built on the premise that your corner of the internet should feel like *you* — not like a generic profile page or a productivity tool with a dashboard you didn't design.

The long-term vision:
- **AI as interior designer, not content author.** Claude designs the space; the owner fills it with their real life. The AI never writes their goals or journal — it sets the atmosphere.
- **A living record.** The kanban, habit tracker, and daily pulse are intended to be updated daily and weekly. The space evolves with the owner over time. After 6 months, a user has 180 pulse entries, 6 AI letters, and a companion who has watched them grow.
- **Beautiful by default.** Every combination of mood + template + AI customisation should look like something the owner would be proud to share. Visual quality is a non-negotiable constraint.
- **Small and personal, not social.** Spaceful is not a social network. There are no follows, feeds, or engagement metrics. The gallery exists to inspire; remix exists to spread; knocks exist to connect — none require an account or feed.
- **AI-assisted, not AI-dependent.** Templates and structure carry most of the experience. AI personalises and synthesises — it does not generate content the user should own. Daily ritual features (pulse, habits) are always free.
- **Irreplaceable over time.** The longer someone uses Spaceful, the more their companion knows them, the richer their journal becomes, the more their space reflects who they are. Switching away means losing that record — not just a pretty page.
